# Medical module guide

Medical owns medical gameplay extensions across Common, Shared, Server, and Client projects. Source projects remain at the repository root.

## Mechanics present

- Body parts, blood stream, wounds, traumas, healing, damage behavior, and body targeting.
- Surgery tools, steps, conditions, effects, and surgery workflows.
- Augments and implant-like tools, autodoc procedures, and item switching.
- Abductor entities and consoles, including client UI and server-side victim/console behavior.
- Part status and wound visuals.

The project-specific guides under this directory show source areas by layer and link representative files. Consult [`CATALOG.md`](../CATALOG.md) for dependencies and build impact. Prototypes and localization live in root `Resources` and are outside the C# directory map.
