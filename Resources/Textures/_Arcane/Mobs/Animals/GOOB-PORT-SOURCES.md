# Goob animal and crop assets

Source: [Goob-Station/Goob-Station](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/tree/cc1d2c911f5cb6c90b36e0899ce918010bd41262), revision `cc1d2c911f5cb6c90b36e0899ce918010bd41262`. All five RSI directories retain the source's CC-BY-SA-3.0 license and copyright metadata. Imported PNG bytes are unchanged.

The four directories `mustard_mothroach.rsi`, `leopard_mothroach.rsi`, `cecropia_mothroach.rsi` and `lunar_mothroach.rsi` moved from `Resources/Textures/_Goobstation/Mobs/Animals/` to this directory. Their original credit is `drown by 51fishstack, made by 4_ydo`. The contribution was introduced by [Goob #6143](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/pull/6143); equipped resources were corrected in [#6543](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/pull/6543). This adaptation retains the six consumed states in each RSI and omits unused icon/lazy/sleep states. Animation timing and pixels are preserved. Fax insertion uses Arcane's existing common mothroach animation.

`Resources/Textures/_Arcane/Objects/Specific/Hydroponics/spaceshroom.rsi` is an unchanged copy of [the source crop RSI](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/tree/cc1d2c911f5cb6c90b36e0899ce918010bd41262/Resources/Textures/_Goobstation/Objects/Specific/Hydroponics/spaceshroom.rsi), introduced by [Goob #7005](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/pull/7005), submitted by charcuteriesampler. Its seed metadata credits a colorized derivative of the source steelcap seed. That [steelcap metadata](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station/blob/cc1d2c911f5cb6c90b36e0899ce918010bd41262/Resources/Textures/Objects/Specific/Hydroponics/steelcap.rsi/meta.json) identifies [TG revision dccc1e60a3c151e2b7fac26da7b3087757f1e116](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/tree/dccc1e60a3c151e2b7fac26da7b3087757f1e116). The [primary license](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/blob/dccc1e60a3c151e2b7fac26da7b3087757f1e116/README.md#license) covers icons under CC-BY-SA-3.0 unless otherwise indicated. No steelcap inhand sprites are imported and their separate artist credit is not attributed to the new growth artwork.

The existing harvested spaceshroom resource, `Objects/Misc/spaceshroom.rsi` (UbaserB, CC0-1.0), is reused without changes. The cultivated crop does not replace that artwork or its attribution.
